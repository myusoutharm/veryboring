Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 19, 2025

South Arm Technology Services Ltd. (d.b.a. Very Boring Technologies) ("we", "us", "our"), operates the website veryboring.ai. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact information — name, email address, phone number, and company name when you submit a form or contact us.
  • Usage data — pages visited, time spent on pages, browser type, device type, and referring URL, collected automatically through cookies and analytics tools.
  • Communications — records of correspondence when you reach out to us via email, phone, or contact forms.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide requested services.
  • To send service-related communications (e.g., project updates, invoices).
  • To improve our website and services through aggregated analytics.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Cookies and Analytics

We use cookies and third-party analytics services (such as Google Analytics and Cloudflare Web Analytics) to understand how visitors use our site. These tools collect data anonymously. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some site features may not function as expected.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services to operate our business, including:

  • Cloudflare — for website security and performance.
  • Google reCAPTCHA — for spam protection on forms.
  • Email and communication platforms — to manage client correspondence.

These providers have their own privacy policies governing how they handle data.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information only when:

  • Required by law or legal process.
  • Necessary to protect our rights or safety.
  • With service providers who assist in operating our business, under confidentiality agreements.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit (TLS/SSL), secure hosting, and access controls. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data.
  • Withdraw consent for data processing.
  • Request a copy of data we hold about you.

To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
