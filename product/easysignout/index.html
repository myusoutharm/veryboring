What it does

One problem, solved well

EasySignOut tracks who has each iPad — nothing more, nothing less. If that's your problem, it'll solve it completely.

Replace the clipboard

Every sign-out is logged automatically — who, which device, when. Searchable history, always accurate.

Staff sign out in seconds

Name, passcode, done. The iOS app on each iPad is the only interface staff need — no logins, no training.

Works across sites

Multiple locations or departments, each with their own isolated view, all managed from one place.

Honest about scope

Is EasySignOut right for you?

We're focused by design. Here's exactly when that's a good thing — and when it isn't.

Great fit

  • 10 to 500 shared iPads, passed between staff or shifts
  • Currently tracking with paper, a spreadsheet, or nothing at all
  • You need it running this week, not next quarter
  • Staff range from very to not at all tech-savvy
  • Schools, clinics, warehouses, field teams, hospitality

Probably not the right fit

  • You need full MDM — app deployment, remote wipe, policy enforcement
  • Thousands of devices across dozens of locations
  • Complex compliance frameworks (SOC 2, HIPAA audit trails, etc.)
  • You need device enrollment and provisioning

For those needs, Jamf, Kandji, or Microsoft Intune are better options. We'll tell you that honestly.

How it works

Set up in days, not months

Four steps. We handle the hard parts. You're signing out iPads by the end of the week.

1

We set up your account

Your isolated tenant, subdomain, and SSO login — ready within 24 hours, no work on your end.

2

Upload your devices and staff

One CSV for iPads, one for employees. Everything is in the system immediately — no manual entry.
