One Friday night, start to finish

Most reservation software is sold on a feature list. Here's what Hoststand does over one busy service instead.

  1. 4:45 pm

    The book is already full of the right bookings.

    Guests booked from your Google listing, the Reserve button on Apple Maps, your Instagram bio and your website. Every channel books against the same tables and the same rules, so nothing gets double-booked.

  2. 5:10 pm

    The phone rings while the host is seating a six-top.

    An AI phone host picks up, checks the real book, holds a table and texts the guest a code to confirm. By the time the host looks up, the booking is on the screen. See how that works.

  3. 5:30 pm

    The kitchen doesn't get 40 covers at once.

    You set how many covers or parties can arrive in each time slot. Once 7:30 is full, online guests only see the times that still have room. The host can still override it for a regular.

  4. 6:05 pm

    A four-top cancels, and nobody has to pick up the phone.

    They tap the link in their confirmation text. The table opens up, and Hoststand can text it to the next party on the waitlist that fits.

  5. 6:40 pm

    Walk-ins join the waitlist at the door.

    They leave a name and a mobile number, then go have a drink. When a table opens they get a text and accept or decline from their phone. No pagers.

  6. 7:20 pm

    A two-top is twenty minutes late.

    The booking turns red on the book. If they call to say they're running late, the host marks it and the table is held until then. If they don't show, one tap marks the no-show and frees the table.

  7. 8:10 pm

    A party of eight walks in.

    Hoststand knows which tables sit next to each other and offers the ones that can be pushed together, not three two-tops at opposite ends of the room.

  8. 11:30 pm

    Close out.

    Covers, no-shows and where each booking came from are on one screen. Every change is recorded with who made it, so "who moved table 12?" has an answer. Export the night to a spreadsheet whenever you want.

Built for AI from the first line of code

Hoststand has a standard door for AI built in: MCP, the Model Context Protocol. Voice agents and AI assistants plug into it and get the same booking engine your host uses, with the limits a public channel needs. Nobody has to give a bot a login to the host screen.

What happens during an AI phone booking

On the phone Inside Hoststand

Guest Do you have a table for four tomorrow, around seven?

check_availability

Returns the times that fit your rules: 7:00, 7:15, 7:45. The AI never sees table numbers or your floor plan, so it can't offer a table you don't sell.

AI I have 7:00 or 7:15. Which works for you?

Guest 7:15. It's Sam, 604-555-0142.

create_hold

Holds a table for a few minutes and texts Sam a six-digit code. Nothing is booked yet. If the call drops, the hold runs out and the table goes back on sale.

Guest The code is 482913.

confirm_reservation

Books it. Sam gets a confirmation text with a link to change or cancel, and the booking shows up on the host's screen with voice AI as its channel.

Guest, next day We're running about fifteen minutes late.

mark_running_late

Holds the table for them and flags it for the host. The phone system tells Hoststand who's calling, so Sam doesn't have to prove anything to say they're late.

What an AI agent can do

  • Answer questions about hours, seating like the patio, and special events
  • Check availability and book, confirmed by a texted code
  • Look up, change or cancel the guest's own booking
  • Tell you a guest is running late
  • Put a guest on the waitlist, check their place, or take them off
  • Give callers a walk-in wait estimate
  • Re-send a lost confirmation link to the caller's phone

What it can't do

  • See table numbers, your floor plan, or anyone else's booking
  • Book tables you keep for walk-ins, or get around your pacing, last-seating and large-party rules
  • Touch a booking without proof it's the guest's: the texted code or the link from their confirmation
  • Seat parties, move tables or mark no-shows. Those stay with your staff.
  • Hammer the system. Attempts are limited per phone number, and wrong codes cancel the hold.
  • Work unseen. Everything it books carries voice AI as its channel, on the booking and in the record of who changed what.

Booking your restaurant shouldn't need an app

Guests pick a date, a party size and a time on your own booking page. There's no app to download and no account to create. They confirm with a code sent by text.

  • One link, everywhere. Put it on your Google listing, the Reserve button on Apple Maps, Instagram and your website.
  • Changes without phone calls. The confirmation text has a link to change the time, the party size, or cancel.
  • Reminders. A text before the booking, with the same link.
  • Full? They don't leave. When there's nothing left, the page offers the waitlist instead of a dead end.

Your rules, the same on every channel

Set them once. The host, the booking page and the phone all follow them.

Service periods
Lunch, dinner, late night, each with its own hours. Late service that runs past midnight works too.
Last seating
No online bookings too close to closing.
Seating pace
A limit on covers or parties arriving in each slot.
Turn times
How long a table is booked for, plus time to reset it.
Walk-in tables
Kept off online and AI booking. The host can still seat them.
Channels
Decide what the booking page, the AI phone host and your staff can each book, by shift, section or table.
Large parties
Ask for your approval before the booking is confirmed.
Events and closures
Block a table, a section or the whole room for a private event or a holiday.
Staff
Staff, manager and owner roles, and a record of who changed what.
Locations
Each location has its own book, floor and hours.

Questions restaurants ask us

Do my guests need to download anything?

No. They book on a web page from their phone or laptop, and everything after that happens by text message.

Do you take a cut of each booking?

No. You pay a flat monthly price per location. Busy months don't cost you more.

Can an AI agent double-book or invent a table?

No. It can only offer times Hoststand gives it, and every booking goes through the same engine and rules as your host. Nothing is booked until the guest enters the code we text them.

Do I have to use your AI phone host?

No. Any voice agent or AI assistant that supports MCP can connect. If you don't have one, VoiceAgent is ours. And if you'd rather keep AI off the phone, leave it off; everything else works the same.

Is my guest list mine?

Yes. You can export your bookings to a spreadsheet at any time. We don't run a diner marketplace, and we don't show your guests other restaurants.

Which POS systems do you work with?

Squirrel today. If you're on something else, tell us which one on the form below. The POS our founding restaurants use is where we go next.

What does it run on?

Any modern browser: an iPad at the host stand, a laptop in the office, a phone on the floor. A native iPad app is in testing.

How does switching work?

We plan it with you. We import your floor, set your hours and rules together, and pick a quiet night to switch over so nothing gets double-booked.

What does it cost?

A flat monthly price per location, quoted on the first call. Founding restaurants keep their rate.
